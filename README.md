Skylark Island Rally — fly the little coral-red Kitefin-9 around Coral Island in a Three.js chase view: thread 8 glowing rings in the Ring Rally, tour 5 sights low over the harbour, or bring her home with one perfect touchdown on Runway 09. Throttle and turn discipline matter, fuel burns, stalls bite, and a hard landing costs you. Keyboard + mouse, lit instruments (altitude, speed, heading, attitude ball, airspeed dial, compass, throttle and fuel), synthesised engine sound, no network, no build step.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Pick a mission (1, 2 or 3), hold W for full throttle, ease back on the arrow keys to rotate, and fly.
